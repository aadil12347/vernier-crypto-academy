## 6. App Mode Direction (`/course` & `/admin`)
- **The Shift:** Students study for 30–120 minutes/session (often nights/NY open) and need instant module switching, note saving, risk calculation, and trade logging without animation fatigue; the Owner inspects orders, approves manual crypto TxIDs, and checks Whop webhook logs rapidly.
- **3 Frequent Jobs:** (1) Watch/read lesson & record timestamped notes; (2) Calculate exact position size & log trade in journal; (3) Verify pending payment / unlock tier.
- **1 High-Stakes Job:** Issuing a refund/revoking access cleanly or approving a `$500` manual crypto transaction without double-granting.
- **Signature Set (10% Cinema Budget):** In `/course#/`, the **Mastery Blueprint Grid** visually tracks all 13 modules across Beginner → Institutional with lit amber focus ONLY on the next uncompleted lesson; in `/admin#/`, the **Pending Review Queue** takes the sole amber focus light when items await approval.
- **All 8 States Wired (`?state=`):** `default`, `loading`, `empty`, `filtered`, `done`, `error`, `denied`, `huge`.
