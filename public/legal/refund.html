Refund & Upgrade Rules

1. Low-Risk Entry Testing. Students can test Lesson 1 previews without payment and evaluate the full Level 1 workflow for $1 before committing $25, $100, or $500.

2. 7-Day Fair Refund Window. Requests submitted within 7 calendar days where less than 25% of newly unlocked tier playbooks have been marked complete are eligible for full refund to original card rail or stablecoin wallet address.

3. Automated Access Revocation. When a refund webhook or admin refund action settles, paid tier entitlements automatically step down to any remaining valid prior tier.
