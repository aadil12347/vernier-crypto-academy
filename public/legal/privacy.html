Data Collection & Telemetry

1. Account Records. We store your email address, full name, country preference, scrypt password hash, lesson completion logs, private study notes, and trade journal entries strictly inside the academy SQLite store.

2. Card Data Isolation. VERNIER never stores raw payment card numbers or CVV codes. Card payments are processed directly inside Whop Checkout's PCI-compliant iframe when enabled, while crypto and local orders record only transaction hashes and reference IDs.

3. Cookie Sessions. Authentication uses a strict HttpOnly session cookie (vernier_sid) without advertising or cross-site behavioral tracking scripts.
